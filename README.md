One sentence about the best meal I ever had: rendang at a roadside stall in Bukittinggi, eaten with my hands while rain hammered the tin roof and the cook kept ladling more onto my rice.

> The best meal I ever had was rendang at a roadside stall in Bukittinggi, eaten with my hands while rain hammered the tin roof and the cook kept ladling more onto my rice.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
